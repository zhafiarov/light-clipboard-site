How to save canned responses on iPhone

A Light Clipboard guide · 20 October 2026

If you answer the same question many times a day from your phone, you want the reply ready, not retyped. iOS has no setting called canned responses or saved replies, but four things get you the same result. Here is what they do, where they fall short, and which fits which job.

1. iOS Text Replacement: free, for short phrases

Text Replacement is part of every iPhone: you give a phrase a short trigger, and typing the trigger expands it.

  1. Open Settings, then General, then Keyboard, then Text Replacement.
  2. Tap the plus. Put the reply in Phrase and a trigger in Shortcut, for example ;addr for your address.
  3. In any app, type the trigger and a space. The phrase replaces it.

Entries sync through iCloud to your iPad and Mac.

Fits when you have a handful of short things: your email address, a greeting, a one-line answer.

Falls short because you must remember each trigger and there is no list to pick from. The Phrase field is built for a line, not a paragraph. A trigger that is also a word fires by accident, so choose ones you would never type otherwise.

2. A note you copy from

Keep a note in Notes, or a draft in Mail, with your replies under headings. When a question comes in, open the note, select the reply, copy, switch back, paste.

Fits when you have a few long replies that go out a couple of times a week, or ones you rewrite heavily each time; length and formatting are no problem.

Falls short because every reply costs two app switches and five or six taps, most of them spent selecting the paragraph with the drag handles, and copying replaces your clipboard. Fine for three replies a day; tedious at thirty.

3. Quick replies inside WhatsApp Business and Instagram

If your customer conversations all happen in one app, that app may already have what you need.

  • WhatsApp Business (not the regular WhatsApp) has Quick Replies under Settings, Business tools. In a chat, type a slash to see the list and tap one.
  • Instagram gives professional accounts Saved Replies, set up in Settings and used from the message box in direct messages.

Fits when everything goes through that one app: a shop that only sells on Instagram, a service that only answers on WhatsApp.

Falls short because each list lives in its own app: a WhatsApp quick reply is no use in Mail, Messages or a web form, so three channels mean three lists. Personal accounts have no such feature.

4. A snippet keyboard, for many replies in many apps

A snippet keyboard is a third-party keyboard whose keys are your saved texts. In any app, hold the globe key, switch to it and tap a tile; the reply is typed into the field. Then switch back and fix a name or a date.

Light Clipboard is one of these: save a reply in the app, give it a name, and it appears as a tile. The keyboard does not ask for Full Access: most keyboards with cloud features do, but this one has no network connection and only reads the snippets you saved (the technical article explains why). To turn it on, open Settings, then General, then Keyboard, then Keyboards, tap Add New Keyboard and choose Light Clipboard.

Fits when you have a dozen or more replies, in several apps, many times a day.

Falls short because switching keyboards is one extra gesture per reply, snippets are plain text with no images, and they stay on the device, so a second device needs its own list.

Which one to pick

  • One or two short phrases: Text Replacement.
  • A few long replies now and then: a note.
  • Every conversation in WhatsApp Business or Instagram: their own quick replies.
  • Many replies, several apps, all day: a snippet keyboard.

They combine well: Text Replacement for your email address, a snippet keyboard for replies that run to a paragraph. For ready-made replies, see saved replies for support, sales and recruiting.

Questions people ask

Does the iPhone have a built-in feature for canned responses?

Not under that name. Text Replacement in Settings is the closest thing: it expands a short trigger into a phrase in any app. Mail has no reply templates, and the quick replies in WhatsApp Business and Instagram work only inside those apps.

Can I use the same saved replies in every app?

Text Replacement works wherever the standard keyboard appears, and so does a snippet keyboard: Mail, Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram, web forms. Quick replies built into one app stay there.

Does a saved-replies keyboard need Full Access?

No. Typing a saved text into a field needs no network and no view of what you type elsewhere. Most keyboards with cloud features ask for Full Access for those features; Light Clipboard does not ask at all. Our text expander guide explains the switch.

Will a saved reply overwrite what is on my clipboard?

From a note, yes: copying the reply replaces what you had copied before. Text Replacement and a snippet keyboard that inserts text through the keyboard leave the clipboard alone.
