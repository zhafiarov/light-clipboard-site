Privacy Policy

Light Clipboard · Last updated 3 October 2026

Light Clipboard collects nothing. It has no account, no server and no analytics. The snippets you save stay on your device.

What the app stores

The text snippets you create are written to storage on your device, in a private container shared between the app and its keyboard. They are not transmitted anywhere, and the developer cannot see them.

What the keyboard does

The Light Clipboard keyboard does not ask for Full Access. It runs in the restricted sandbox iOS gives a keyboard by default, which lets it read the snippets the app saved and nothing else.

The keyboard does not record, store or transmit anything you type. It makes no network requests of any kind.

What is not collected

  • No personal information, name or email address
  • No keystrokes, no typing history, no clipboard contents beyond snippets you deliberately save
  • No usage analytics, crash telemetry or advertising identifiers
  • No location, contacts, photos or device identifiers

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app receives only whether an entitlement is active. Payment details are handled by Apple and never reach this app or its developer. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Writing to the developer

If you email the developer, from your own mail app or with Contact the Developer in the app, the developer receives your email address and what you write, and uses them only to answer you. A message started from the app already names the app's version, the iOS version and whether the device is an iPhone or an iPad; you can delete any of it before sending. The app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes every snippet along with it. Because nothing is stored off the device, there is no other copy to request or erase.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
